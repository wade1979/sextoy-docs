# How Motion Works

Pulsar uses Motion Intelligence™ to turn human-inspired stimulation techniques into consistent, adaptive device movement.

## Inspired by Real Human Techniques

<img
  src="/images/guide/motion-intelligence/technique-archetypes.png"
  alt="Technique archetypes used for motion analysis"
  width="720"
/>

Pulsar motion patterns are not random.

We studied dozens of real-world stimulation techniques and extracted their movement characteristics through motion analysis.

Each technique produces a unique rhythm, pacing profile, and stimulation style.

More than 40 technique archetypes were analyzed.

## Beyond Fixed Patterns

<img
  src="/images/guide/motion-intelligence/traditional-vs-pulsar.png"
  alt="Traditional fixed patterns compared with Pulsar adaptive motion sessions"
  width="760"
/>

Traditional devices ask:

“Which mode would you like to repeat?”

Pulsar asks:

“What should happen next?”

Powered by human-inspired motion signatures, AI orchestration, and preference learning, Pulsar continuously generates adaptive motion sessions in real time.

Rather than looping the same pattern, the system builds a dynamic experience that evolves throughout the session — creating more variation, more personalization, and a more natural sense of progression.

## Pulsar Learns. Adapts. Evolves.

### The More You Use It, The More Personal It Becomes

Every interaction tells Pulsar a little more about your preferences.

A quick tap on Like.

A request to slow down.

A favorite rhythm you return to again and again.

These signals help build a dynamic preference profile that evolves with you over time.

Rather than replaying the same experience, Pulsar continuously adapts future sessions — creating motion, pacing, and progression that feel increasingly tailored to you.
