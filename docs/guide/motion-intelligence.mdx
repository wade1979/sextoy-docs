# How Motion Works

Pulsar uses Motion Intelligence™ to turn expert human motion into reusable motion signatures, then uses AI to arrange those signatures into adaptive session timelines.

## Expert Technique Learning

We study real-world motion data and identify representative motion segments that capture distinct rhythms, pacing, and progression. These carefully selected motion seeds are transformed into Motion Signatures—the reusable building blocks that power the Pulsar AI Motion Engine.

## Real-time AI Choreography

Combined with real-time feedback and learned preferences, these signatures are continuously blended, adapted, and orchestrated to create sessions that evolve in real time. No single signature defines the experience. It is the way they are combined and adapted that makes every session unique.

Traditional devices ask:

“Which mode would you like to repeat?”

Pulsar asks:

“What should happen next?”

Rather than looping the same pattern, the system builds a dynamic experience that evolves throughout the session — creating more variation, more personalization, and a more natural sense of progression.

## Pulsar Learns. Adapts. Evolves.

### The More You Use It, The More Personal It Becomes

Every interaction tells Pulsar a little more about your preferences.

A quick tap on Like.

A request to slow down.

A favorite rhythm you return to again and again.

These signals help build a dynamic preference profile that evolves with you over time.

Rather than replaying the same experience, Pulsar continuously adapts future sessions — creating motion, pacing, and progression that feel increasingly tailored to you.
