Watch. Feel. Immerse.

`Video Sync` turns compatible video content into a synchronized interactive experience. Whether the user is watching a local video, streaming content from a supported platform, or enjoying immersive VR scenes, Pulsar aligns device motion with what is happening on screen to create a deeper sense of presence and realism.

## Before You Choose a Source

Local video and audio stay on your device, while selected derived signals are sent to the motion engine. Uploaded Funscript files are processed on the server. Session-derived signals, automatically generated motor commands and uploaded scripts are deleted after the session ends. See [local-video and Funscript data handling](/legal/ai-data-practices/#local-video-processing).

For buying decisions, check [Funscript motion behavior](https://getpulsar.io/support/technical-faq#funscript-motion) and [your browser, headset or player combination](https://getpulsar.io/support/technical-faq#browsers-vr-players). Local-video data practices do not establish the same path for online video, VR or live calls.

## Supported Content Sources

- Local videos, including 2D, VR180, and VR360 content
- Supported streaming platforms
- Shared video links, including YouTube, DeoVR, and more
- User-created motion scripts in Funscript format

## Intelligent Motion Synchronization

Pulsar offers two ways to drive motion: AI Motion Sync and Script-Based Sync.

### AI Motion Sync

AI Motion Sync analyzes the content, the user's interaction history, and personal preferences to generate motion patterns that feel natural and engaging.

Over time, Pulsar learns:

- preferred intensity
- preferred pacing
- favorite motion styles
- session habits

Each experience becomes increasingly personalized.

### Script-Based Sync

Video Sync supports uploaded standard Funscript files. The server motion engine dynamically converts scripted position changes into executable motion, primarily following rhythm and timing rather than strict position tracking. Smoothing, movement merging and speed limits can change details. See [how Funscript motion works](https://getpulsar.io/support/technical-faq#funscript-motion) and [script upload and deletion](/legal/ai-data-practices/#uploaded-funscript).

Pulsar supports the industry-standard Funscript format and is compatible with content created by thousands of creators across the global scripting community.

Useful community entry points:

- [OpenFunscripter Community](https://github.com/OpenFunscripter/OFS/discussions)
- [Eroscripts](https://discuss.eroscripts.com/)

## Immersive VR Experience

Video Sync can provide synchronized motion inside immersive VR environments. When paired with VR180 or VR360 content, Pulsar combines visual immersion, synchronized motion, and adaptive AI control to create a more engaging and realistic experience.
