# Contact Support

Contact support for preorder questions, product access issues, warranty requests, return questions, and troubleshooting that is not covered in the guide.

## Before Contacting Support

Please prepare:

- Order number or preorder confirmation
- Email address used for purchase or activation
- Device token or product identifier, if available
- Description of the issue
- Screenshots, photos, or videos that help explain the problem

## Support Requests

Use the official support channel provided on the purchase page, order confirmation, or product packaging. Support contact details will be updated here when the public support channel is finalized.
