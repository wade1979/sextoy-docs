# Warranty Policy

This warranty policy explains what customers can expect if a product has a manufacturing defect or stops working under normal use.

## Limited Warranty Coverage

The product is covered by a limited warranty against defects in materials and workmanship. Warranty coverage applies only to products purchased through an authorized sales channel.

## What the Warranty Covers

- Manufacturing defects
- Hardware failures under normal use
- Problems caused by defective materials or assembly

## What the Warranty Does Not Cover

- Damage caused by misuse, accidents, unauthorized modification, or improper cleaning
- Normal wear and cosmetic changes that do not affect product function
- Damage caused by using the product outside the instructions in the user guide
- Products purchased from unauthorized resellers

## Warranty Service

If a product may be eligible for warranty service, contact support with the order number, product identifier, a description of the issue, and any relevant photos or videos. Support will review the request and provide the next steps.

## Preorder Note

During the preorder period, warranty terms may be finalized before general shipment. The latest published warranty terms will apply when the product ships.
