# Support

Purchase support, warranty and return policies are maintained on the [Pulsar website](https://getpulsar.io/support/contact-support). This guide focuses on product setup, use and troubleshooting.

:::important Before purchase

Please review [Returns & Exchanges](https://getpulsar.io/support/returns-exchanges) before ordering. Due to hygiene requirements for intimate products, opened or used products are not eligible for return or refund for personal preference, dissatisfaction, or change of mind when there is no verified quality issue.

:::
