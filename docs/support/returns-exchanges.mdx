# Returns & Exchanges

This policy explains how returns and exchanges are handled during the preorder and early access period.

## Preorder Cancellations

If the product has not shipped, customers may contact support to request a preorder cancellation. Cancellation availability may depend on payment status, fulfillment preparation, and the sales channel used for purchase.

## Returns

Return eligibility depends on product condition, hygiene requirements, purchase channel, and applicable law. Products must be handled according to the product instructions and returned only through the process provided by support.

## Exchanges

If a product arrives with a verified defect or shipping-related issue, support may offer an exchange, replacement, or other remedy after reviewing the request.

## How to Request Help

Contact support with the order number, email address used for purchase, a description of the issue, and any relevant photos or videos. Do not ship a product back unless support has provided return instructions.
