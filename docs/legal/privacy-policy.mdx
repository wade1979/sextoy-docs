# Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Pulsar respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

## Information We Collect

We may collect:

- Account information, such as email address and login credentials
- Device information, such as device ID, firmware version, and connection status
- Usage information, such as feature usage, preferences, and app interactions
- Support information you voluntarily provide when contacting customer support

## How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Provide and improve our products and services
- Enable device connectivity and account functionality
- Deliver software and firmware updates
- Respond to support requests
- Maintain security and prevent abuse

## AI Features

If AI-powered features are used, information may be processed to provide personalized responses, preferences, or experiences. Details are described in our [AI & Data Practices](/legal/ai-data-practices/) document.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

We may share information with trusted service providers that help us operate our services, including hosting, payment processing, analytics, customer support, and AI service providers.

## Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information.
