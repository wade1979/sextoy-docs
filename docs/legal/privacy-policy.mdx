# Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how personal information is collected, used, and protected when customers visit the website, place a preorder, contact support, or use the product web app.

## Information We May Collect

We may collect information such as:

- Contact information, including email address
- Order and preorder information
- Device access information, such as device token or product identifier
- Support request details
- Basic website and service usage information

## How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

- Process preorders and customer requests
- Provide product access and activation
- Respond to support questions
- Improve the product, documentation, and web app
- Maintain service reliability and prevent misuse

## Sharing Information

Personal information is not sold as part of the preorder experience. Information may be shared with service providers that help operate the website, process orders, deliver support, or maintain the service.

## Cookies and Analytics

The website may use cookies or similar technologies for basic functionality, analytics, and service improvement. Cookie and analytics practices may be updated as the product and website evolve.

## Your Privacy Rights

Customers may contact support to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information where applicable. Some information may need to be retained for order, support, legal, security, or accounting reasons.

## Updates

This policy may be updated as the product moves from preorder to general availability.
