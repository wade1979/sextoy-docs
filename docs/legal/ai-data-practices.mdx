# AI & Data Practices

Last Updated: June 2026

This document explains how Pulsar's AI-powered features use and process information.

## AI Interactions

AI features may process user inputs, preferences, interaction history, and selected settings in order to provide personalized experiences.

## AI Memory

Some AI features may remember preferences, interaction patterns, or user-selected settings to improve future experiences.

Users may be given options to modify or reset certain remembered preferences.

## Model Providers

AI functionality may be provided by Pulsar and/or trusted third-party AI service providers.

## Training and Model Improvement

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, personal conversations and interaction content are not used to train public AI models.

## Live Sync Features

Live Sync and real-time communication features are designed to transmit information necessary for the session.

Live video streams are not recorded or stored by Pulsar by default.

## User Control

Users remain responsible for the information they choose to share during AI interactions, live sessions, or companion experiences.

## Changes

As AI capabilities evolve, this document may be updated to reflect new features and practices.
