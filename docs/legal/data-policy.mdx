# Data Policy

This data policy summarizes how product-related data is handled during the preorder and early access period.

## Product Access Data

The product may use device tokens, email activation, and service status information to enable access and confirm product entitlements.

## Support Data

Support requests may include order information, contact details, device identifiers, screenshots, photos, videos, and written descriptions of the issue. This information is used to investigate and resolve customer requests.

## Data Retention

Data is retained only as long as needed for product access, customer support, service operation, legal obligations, fraud prevention, or business records.

## Data Protection

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect product, preorder, and support data. No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

## Preorder Note

Data practices may be refined before general availability. Any material updates will be reflected in the published privacy and data policies.
